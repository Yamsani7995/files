About

Finding signal in the noise.

I'm a third-year BTech CSE student with a focus on cybersecurity, currently working through a self-directed study plan that spans security, applied AI/ML and game development. I like projects that force me to go deep rather than wide — reading protocols closely, breaking things on purpose, then rebuilding them properly.

Outside coursework I build mobile apps end-to-end — mobile client, backend, and the AI services in between — and I'm working on a long-term multimodal AI project for the Telugu language. Longer term, I want to build a company somewhere in the overlap of cybersecurity, AI and product.
